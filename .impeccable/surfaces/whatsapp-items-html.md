# Surface: whatsapp-items.html

Generated by `generate_whatsapp.py`. Never hand-edited.

## Scope and mode

Persuade. One page, one job: a neighbour decides which item they want and messages for it.

This surface runs its own visual world and does not inherit DESIGN.md, which governs `index.html` only. It is registered in the detector's `ignoreFiles` for exactly that reason.

## Audience and job

Neighbours in the building's Free and For Sale WhatsApp chats, arriving from a chat link, almost always on a phone. They skim roughly twenty items, judge condition from the photographs, and message in the chat. Ranked jobs, confirmed with Kyle: scan and pick fast on a phone; tell free from for-sale instantly; judge condition from the photographs.

## Direction

A packing manifest: numbered lines on a form, refusing the product grid of equal-weight cards. Neutral form stock, one black ink, and a dispatch-orange marking ink spent only on what is free. Archivo at normal proportions. Rules carry every division; no shadows, no rounded cards, no color fields.

Memorable moment: an opened line feeds its photographs out in a short staggered clip-path cascade, like paper leaving a printer.

Kyle chose the quietest of four rendered intensities: paper header rather than a solid ink band, no stretched letterforms, no sprocket margin, roomier rows.

## Constraints

- No contact link. A `wa.me` link would publish a phone number on a page open to the internet, and the audience is already in the chat. Per-line permalinks carry the same benefit with no exposure
- Static, no build step for the served page, no runtime dependencies
- Light and dark via `prefers-color-scheme`
- Free versus for-sale must be readable without color alone: the section, the price column, and the word "Free" all carry it
